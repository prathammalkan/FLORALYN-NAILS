Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Anything else? Email us at floralyyn7@gmail.com and we'll reply same day.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Floralyn is a by-appointment-only home studio. Walk-ins aren't possible since I book specific time slots for each client. Please book through the form below at least 24 hours in advance, or email us directly.

How long does gel nail art last?

Gel nail art typically lasts 2–4 weeks depending on your daily routine and how well you care for your nails. Avoiding prolonged water exposure and applying cuticle oil daily helps significantly. I use gel brands chosen specifically for durability.

Can I bring my own inspiration?

Absolutely — reference photos are always welcome. Share them in the booking form or mention them in the notes field. Every design is drawn freehand and adapted to suit your nail shape and length, so the result is custom to you even if the inspiration comes from somewhere else.

What is the price range?

Starting prices: Simple manicure from ₹300 · Gel nails from ₹700 · Custom nail art from ₹1,200 · Bridal sets from ₹2,000. The final price depends on complexity, nail length, and design detail. An exact quote is shared when you book.

Do you do nail extensions?

Currently Floralyn focuses on gel nail art on natural nails. Nail extensions and acrylics may be added in the future — follow on Instagram to stay updated.

How far in advance should I book?

For regular appointments, 2–3 days ahead is usually enough. For bridal sets or special occasions, please book at least 2–4 weeks in advance to lock in your preferred slot — these fill up fast.
