Do you accept walk-ins?

Floralyn is by-appointment only. I book specific time slots for each client, so walk-ins aren't possible. Please book through the form below at least 24 hours in advance.

How long does gel nail art last?

Typically 2–4 weeks depending on daily activities and nail care. Avoiding prolonged water exposure and applying cuticle oil regularly makes a big difference. I choose gel brands specifically for durability.

Can I bring my own inspiration?

Always welcome. Share Pinterest boards or Instagram screenshots in the booking form or via email. Every design is drawn freehand and adapted to your nail shape and length.

What are the prices?

Simple manicure from ₹300 · Gel nails from ₹700 · Custom nail art from ₹1,200 · Bridal from ₹2,000. Final price depends on complexity and nail length — exact quote shared on booking.

Do you do nail extensions?

Currently Floralyn focuses on gel nail art on natural nails. Extensions may be added in the future — follow on Instagram for updates.

How far in advance should I book?

2–3 days for regular appointments. For bridal or special occasions, book 2–4 weeks ahead to secure your slot — these fill up quickly.
