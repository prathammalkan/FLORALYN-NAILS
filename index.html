48 Hours Before

Pre-Appointment Prep

Skip clipping cuticles or soaking in oily baths right before your slot — dry prep allows builder gel to bond seamlessly for 3–4 weeks without lifting.

Weeks 1 – 4

Daily Velvet Glow

Massage a drop of sweet almond or jojoba oil around cuticles each night and slip on gloves for lengthy dishwashing so top-coat gloss stays mirror-clear.

Fresh Canvas

Gentle Soak-Off Removal

Never peel cured gel off natural keratin layers — book a gentle steam/foil soak-off when your set grows out to keep your nail plate thick and healthy.

Studio FAQ

Do you accept walk-ins?

Floralyn works strictly by appointment so every client receives unhurried 1-on-1 chair attention. Please request your slot online at least 24 hours in advance.

How long does gel nail art last?

With gentle builder reinforcement on natural nails, sets typically wear cleanly for 2–4 weeks depending on nail growth and daily care. Nightly cuticle oil helps keep flexibility high and edges chip-free.

Can I bring Pinterest inspiration or my wedding outfit palette?

Yes — bring saved references, lehenga embroidery photos, or blend a vibe in our Studio Polish Bar above. Every set is painted freehand and scaled thoughtfully to flatter your nail bed.

What is the price range at Floralyn?

Simple manicures start from ₹300, gel sets from ₹700, custom freehand nail art from ₹1,200, and bespoke bridal sets from ₹2,000. You receive a clear quote before your brush even touches paint.

Do you do acrylic extensions?

Floralyn focuses exclusively on soft & builder gel artistry over your natural nails so your natural keratin stays strong, flexible, and healthy.

How far ahead should brides or festive clients book?

2–3 days ahead is ideal for everyday gel or custom sets, and 2–4 weeks prior for bridal or wedding functions so we can reserve an unhurried two-hour studio slot.
